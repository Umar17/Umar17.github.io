"What an overrated city...", was the first impression seeing Istanbul through my taxi on my way to the hotel from the Airport. And this thought developed even more through the second morning when I walked a bit with no navigation and a chaos of traffic on the road. Still, I stayed there for a week and I realised Istanbul is not a place to judge in an hour or in a day, you really need months to know what Istanbul do to you. It was one of the toughest goodbyes among my travels.
